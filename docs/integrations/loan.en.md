# Loan module

Status: The shared pool is published. Loans use game ISK. There is one fixed shared pool: personal or corporation contributors submit amounts to it, and the borrower is a site member account. All characters bound to one account share its loan responsibility.

Interest follows a familiar loan mechanism: a manager configures an annual nominal rate in basis points (100 bps = 1%), and each application calculates fixed simple interest once as `principal × annual rate × installment count × interval days ÷ (10000 × 365)`, rounded once to ISK minor units. Principal and interest are stored separately and split across equal installments; the final installment absorbs rounding. The annual rate is snapshotted with the application, so policy changes never recalculate existing loans. A contribution is pending until the contributor sends a completed cash-only contract to the configured personal custodian character or corporation. The amount, direction, acceptor and empty item set are checked and the global contract claim succeeds before the amount becomes pool cash. Approval is not disbursement; disbursement also reserves and debits available pool cash after contract verification.

The system evaluates the credit score, total responsibility limit, unsecured limit, and rule version from loan evidence; administrators cannot edit these values. Personal base credit also reads verified personal-wallet income snapshots for the account's active bound characters, converts net income in a configurable observation window using the debt ratio, term, and score factor, and applies a personal hard cap. If there are too few samples, the default is to fall back to the verified pool-cash calculation; managers can switch this to suspend personal credit. Wallet authorization only controls evidence availability and is never a score input. The final application remains limited by shared-pool available cash. `system-v2` weights repayment at 55, leverage at 20, guarantee/collateral history at 10, PAP score at 5, and asset-scale score at 10. Authorization state and account age are never score factors. PAP is split into two factors: the closed-event corporation activity attendance rate over the last year (3 points) and the share of complete alliance PAP snapshots in the last six months that meet the configured monthly requirement (2 points). Each factor is proportionally rounded and capped. Missing activity records, missing alliance snapshots, or incomplete syncs remain neutral rather than becoming a zero-score penalty. Asset-scale scoring is enabled: the service reads personal `esi-assets.read_assets.v1` holdings for every active character bound to the account and values the aggregate with EVE's global average-price table; corporation assets and wallet balances are excluded. The total is scored as 0 below 100 million ISK, then 2, 4, 6, 8, and 10 points at 100 million, 500 million, 1 billion, 5 billion, and 10 billion ISK. If an asset page, price quote, or authorization is incomplete, the whole factor remains neutral instead of scoring a partial snapshot. The evidence retains its observation time and snapshot identifier. Authorization only controls whether evidence can be read or refreshed. A default suspends the credit state. The total responsibility limit uses the shared pool's verified cash balance as its principal-pool base and is recalculated from the current score; unverified contribution promises and website balances are excluded. Each approval records an immutable evaluation snapshot. Guarantee and collateral coverage are rechecked in the approval transaction. A guarantee amount is limited by both the configured share of the loan principal and the guarantor's current available total-responsibility capacity. Acceptance rechecks the total responsibility remainder in a transaction before reserving it. Collateral remains pending until an administrator approves a completed item-exchange contract whose item snapshot is read and frozen by the server; it is released when the loan settles. The module never auto-debits wallets, liquidates collateral, or treats a wallet balance as payment evidence.

The shared pool is a fixed singleton. The module migration creates one open corporation pool from the corporation with the largest known character profile set; the page does not expose pool creation, switching, or pool limit configuration, and members submit amounts only. A loan manager selects a currently readable personal character wallet, or an authorized division of the shared pool corporation wallet, as the funding wallet, and selects an actively bound personal EVE character as the contract officer. Incoming, disbursement, and repayment contract ownership is checked against the selected wallet owner; the officer remains the management and operator configuration. Verified loan ledger entries remain the only source of lendable cash; a wallet snapshot is configuration evidence and is never treated as payment evidence. No deposit verification is allowed without a valid officer and funding wallet. Unverified promises and website balances are never treated as cash. Covered collateral cannot exceed valuation multiplied by the published haircut policy, and a submitted haircut must match that policy.

Loan management is assigned through the site's permission roles; selecting an EVE contract officer does not grant loan management access. The account must first receive `corporation.loan`, then an authorized manager can select its actively bound personal EVE character.

Payment verification uses the existing EVE contract cache service. Disbursement requires a finished contract, exact amount, and the borrower receiving character. Repayment may be partial and is allocated from the earliest unpaid installment while preserving principal and interest detail. One in-game contract can be claimed by only one delivery module; retries do not credit twice.

The module exposes the authenticated `loan.self` routes described in the Chinese guide, including contribution submission and automatic verification against recent synchronized finished contracts. `GET /api/v1/loan/guarantees/candidates` returns selectable active character names; invitation creation resolves the account again. Corporation contributions and custody configuration additionally check `corporation.loan`. Goose migrations 71–73 and 79–82 add the shared-pool contribution, cash ledger, credit evaluation tables, rate snapshots, and the fixed shared-pool seed. The page appears when `loan` is enabled in `MODULES`.

The policy endpoints are `GET/POST /api/v1/loan/settings/policy` and `GET /api/v1/loan/evaluations`. Policy writes are versioned and manager-only; evaluation history is scoped to the current account.

Pending corporation-pool applications are also exposed as the `loan` source in the approval center. The center is read-only and reuses this module's review endpoint; personal lender decisions stay on the loan page.

The loan module uses two navigation pages: `/loans` is the Loan dashboard and shows the shared-pool overview; `/loans/operations` is Loan operations and contains applications, contributions, guarantees, collateral, and loan records, with Loan dashboard, Loan operations, Risk settings, and Evaluation history tabs. Beneath the tab switcher, the Loan operations tab shows only the caller's credit score and personal limit; score factors are not displayed. Risk settings is manager-only and configures the annual rate, income window, debt ratio, term, score factor, personal cap, sample threshold, evidence freshness, guarantee/collateral caps, funding wallet, and contract officer. Evaluation history shows the caller's immutable approval snapshots. Loan settings are visible only to loan managers, who can select the officer from all active bound EVE characters rather than only their own account. Contributions are shown as compact list rows with lender character, contribution type, amount and state; clicking a row opens its detail modal. A pending detail shows only the copyable fields needed to create the contract: the loan officer character name, payment amount and contract note. The character ID remains in the API and backend for validation; the page falls back to the ID only when the name is temporarily unavailable. The contract type and no-item requirement are explanatory text. After synchronization, the system automatically verifies a matching finished contract; the page provides no verification button. Lender and receiving characters are selected by nickname from the current account's bound-character dropdown; the submitted character ID is supplied by the system. New applications offer repayment intervals of 1, 3, 7, 14, or 30 days, and the backend rejects other intervals. The first repayment date is generated after verified disbursement as disbursement time plus the selected interval, then remaining installments use the same interval. Guarantee invitations use a selectable active character list. Collateral uses a selectable finished item-exchange contract list, derives coverage from the configured haircut, and keeps server-side contract revalidation. The operations page shows only the system credit score and personal limit; score factors are not displayed. Asset-scale scoring uses the complete personal ESI snapshot described above; wallet balances are not a substitute and incomplete asset evidence remains neutral. Corporation contributions require the corporation permission. Approval re-evaluates credit, cash, guarantees, and collateral before accepting an application. The Loan business dashboard links clearly to operations; the operations page always shows a “My guarantee invitations” section, where a guarantor can review the loan principal and guarantee amount, then accept or reject an invitation. Acceptance rechecks the current total responsibility capacity.

Real Tranquility contract verification, direction, partial repayment, duplicate claims, and authorization expiry still require production acceptance; deployment and module enablement are not evidence that the real contract loop is accepted.
